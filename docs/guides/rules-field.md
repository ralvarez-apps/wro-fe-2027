## Open questions

- Rack unit Fig 4 labels 225, 135, 120, 75, 75, 15 mm: which is level height, spacing, leg height, shelf thickness? Not stated in text (p.9).
- 300 vs 330 mm: inner vs outer width? 120 mm depth shown by arrow, confirm (p.9).
- Rack unit width vs row 960 mm: shared uprights make 3 x 330 not equal 960; derive carefully (p.9).
- Rack row orientation/position along the long axis: only gaps in Fig 3; row-to-top-wall and workbench-to-wall distances not labelled (p.8).
- Whether 563/400/400/563 mm span the 2362 mm axis and what row depth is: sum check needed (p.8).
- Workbench exact position (centre offset), 720 mm label vs Workbench Zone size (p.8, p.13).
- Charging station positions (corner offsets), yellow area size, 400 mm reference, 100 mm labels meaning (p.12).
- Workbench Zone and Storage Zone boundary coordinates (p.13-14).
- Order list area positions and numbering order relative to Workbench (p.14).
- Mat artwork/colours (decorative graphics) not specified (p.5).
- Box internal geometry (openings, protrusions) not dimensioned in text (p.11).
- Wall thickness, wall hazard stripes seen in Fig 3 (yellow/black on side walls): not described in text (p.8).
